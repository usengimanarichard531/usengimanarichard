About Me

I am a data scientist with a strong mathematical foundation, currently pursuing my Master of Science in Mathematical Sciences with a focus on Data Science at the African Institute for Mathematical Sciences (AIMS) in Kigali, Rwanda.

My work sits at the intersection of mathematical theory and practical application. With expertise in statistical machine learning, big data analysis, and mathematical modeling, I transform complex datasets into actionable insights that drive real-world impact.

I am particularly interested in applying advanced machine learning techniques to challenges in developing contexts, leveraging data science for social good, and contributing to the growing field of AI research across Africa.
